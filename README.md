### LAB 14 – Capstone GenAI Workflow Portfolio

This lab focused on applying the learning from previous Generative AI activities to a practical aviation-business project. The selected project was **“Travel Smart at the Airport — GenAI Passenger-Awareness Campaign.”** The main business problem was that passengers may depend on incomplete or unverified travel information before arriving at the airport. The campaign was designed to encourage passengers to check official airline and airport information before travelling.

The project started with a **business brief** covering the problem, target audience, objective, required outputs, privacy considerations, compliance requirements, and human decision-making responsibilities. The target audience was domestic airline passengers. The planned outputs included a social-media post, passenger-awareness poster, presentation visual, awareness email, and brand-and-accuracy checklist.

A proposed **GenAI workflow** was developed from the business brief through tool selection, prompt design, AI generation, human review, source verification, correction, final approval, and passenger communication. Different tools were selected according to the business requirement. ChatGPT was used for prompting and workplace writing, Canva for visual design, Gamma/Canva for presentation development, an approved image-generation tool for visual concepts, document-grounded AI for source verification, and GitHub for portfolio documentation.

The lab also created a **prompt portfolio** showing how simple prompts were improved by adding role, context, task, audience, constraints, format, and verification instructions. The generated content was reviewed for accuracy, tone, compliance, context, privacy, and visual quality.

A **hallucination audit, privacy audit, verification trail, and severity × risk triage** were completed. Unsupported baggage rules, guaranteed queue reductions, fake statistics, incorrect security information, and operational promises were removed or marked for verification. The Claim → Source → Match → Context → Action method was used to verify important claims.

The project concluded with a 12-slide presentation, GitHub portfolio structure, peer-review section, and individual reflection. Overall, the lab demonstrated that **Generative AI can support aviation communication and content development, but human judgement, source verification, privacy protection, and final approval remain essential**.
